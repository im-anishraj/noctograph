# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, threats, sustained disruption, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
